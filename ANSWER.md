# Should we keep spending on campaigns?

Do not keep this budget as it is.

We spent **€407,500** on twelve campaigns. On the completed orders linked to those campaigns, the money left after paying our suppliers was **€114,103.34**. That is **€293,396.66 less** than we spent.

Keep four small campaigns, at the same size as this year. Stop the other eight. Do not run Summer Mega Sale again.

"Money left" means a completed sale only: what the customer paid after the discount, minus what we pay the supplier for the goods. Returned orders and cancelled orders are not included. Each campaign's advertising bill is taken off once.
